The test dip

The best place to shop for a hot tub is in one.

Every body fits a seat differently. A spec sheet can't tell you whether a lounger is too long or a jet hits the right spot. Twenty minutes in the water can.

  1. Pick a time, or just stop by

    Book a test dip below or give us a call. Walk-ins are welcome during showroom hours.

  2. Bring a swimsuit and a towel

    Climb in and try the seats. Swap JetPaks, find the depth that fits you, and see how the lights and controls work.

  3. Get an honest plan

    We walk through delivery, electrical, your pad or deck, and financing. Transparent pricing, zero pressure.
